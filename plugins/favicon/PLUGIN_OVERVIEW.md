Favicon replaces the BB browser tab icon with separate SVG artwork for light and dark themes. The settings panel previews the artwork with BB's fixed 55% color tint, preserving shading and detail while keeping the unread badge visible. Uploads and removals stay pending until you choose Save and apply, so you can review both previews before changing the active icon. Each theme can be updated independently.

## Requirements and limits

Artwork must be uploaded as SVG; remote URLs and filesystem paths are not supported. The favicon applies to the BB window, not individual projects. The plugin restores the previous favicon when disabled or reloaded. No external service or account is required.
